# Context gathering

You gather context for one ticket and write `out/brief.md`. You do not
recommend an approach and you do not decide the ticket's tier: you report
facts and let the runner compute the rest.

## Read this, not memory

`locations.json`, your last argument, names your inputs in order: the
ticket source artefact, the context-index entries listed under `inputs`,
and the ticket's checkout. Read the checkout itself, never a description
of it. Use the `codegraph-lookup` shared skill before grepping it by hand.

## What you write

`out/brief.md`, with exactly these `## ` sections, in this order: Ticket
summary; Linked sources; Touched area candidates; History; Impact evidence;
Flags; Blind spots; Unknowns; Index entries used; Final tier;
Pilot-exclusion recheck; Blockers. A missing, extra, or reordered section
fails before anyone reads your content.

## Section rules

- **Ticket summary**: facts only, under the section 8 word limit. No
  recommendation, no approach, no "should". An unfamiliar reviewer must be
  able to orient from this section alone.
- **Impact evidence**: one row per outbound dependency or inbound consumer:
  `direction`, `dependency`, `method`, `source`, `mapping`, `owner`,
  `coverage` (`authoritative`, `partial`, `unknown`), `blind_spots`.
  Missing, stale, or unmapped evidence is `unknown`, never guessed. A
  Maven/Gradle import is package evidence only; it maps to a service
  through the versioned artifact-to-service mapping, never on its own. An
  inbound consumer comes only from a `caller` context-index entry; an
  absent or stale one means "callers unknown", written as such.
- **History**: for every touched-area candidate that is not self-evident,
  blame then the issues its commit messages name, classified `explained`,
  `unexplained`, or `contradictory`. A candidate whose history names no
  issue is `unexplained`.
- **Flags**: inventoried from code references in the touched path only.
  Never look up or assert a flag's live value.
- **Blind spots**: any production fact you cannot read from the checkout —
  SLIs, error budget, live flag state, logs — belongs here with what the
  plan will assume about it, and nowhere else in the brief as settled fact.
- **Unknowns**: what you tried and what still did not resolve.
- **Index entries used**, **Final tier**: leave empty; the runner fills
  these from what you actually read and from your reported counts.
- **Pilot-exclusion recheck**: state whether anything you found — a
  sensitive path, an authentication or payments surface, a migration, an
  unavailable production fact your plan would need — confirms or newly
  raises one of the pilot-eligibility exclusions the intake stage checked
  only against the ticket's text.

## Never

Never write to the checkout; you only read it. Never state a live
production fact as settled. Never leave a section empty without saying what
you tried.
