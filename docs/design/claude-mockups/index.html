Direction 01

Workbench

Open

A clean operations workbench. A dark charcoal sidebar stands against a white content area; everything else is hairline borders and dense, readable tables, with floating layers exactly where a person acts — a detail card over the queue list, a dark bulk bar under a selection. One accent, deep green in light and lime on dark, with semantic colour reserved strictly for outcomes, states and buckets. Light-first, and the dark theme is tuned rather than inverted: elevation comes from lighter surfaces, borders sit at 8–17% white.

Direction 02

Observatory

Open

A monitoring room. Near-black surfaces, cards separated by 1px hairlines at 8% white, small tabular numerals, a sparkline wherever a number lives, and a live activity rail on the two screens where work is in flight. Status colour carries all the meaning; nothing is coloured for decoration. Dark-first, and the light theme is a tuned off-white console — each semantic hue re-picked against white rather than inverted, so a chip reads with the same weight in both themes.

Direction 03

Canvas

Open

The factory drawn as a node canvas. A ticket's S0 to S6 walk is a graph of node cards on a dotted canvas, with labelled bezier edges for the artefacts that flow between stages and the review gate drawn as an approved / send-back branch; a console and a per-stage debug panel sit beneath it, the manifest becomes a node library, and the tables live inside frosted cards. Light-first, with glass used sparingly — 72% white cards over a soft gradient in light, 85% #1A1D24 over #0F1115 in dark, always with a 1px border so text stays crisp.
