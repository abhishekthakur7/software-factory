## 3. Design simplifications applied in v0.2

These changed the diagrams and the register only; no row changed.

- The stage drivers no longer chain to each other; C2 dispatches each and every driver reports back through the record (charter C7).
- The checks and gates are one component (C9) invoked at the points the rows name, instead of an exclusion gate drawn three times and a preflight drawn in three places.
- The reviewer-set derivation, the question gate and the list-view commands are drawn once, in the control plane; domain 1 is the person's view.
- Recipes are the launcher's typed-command surface plus one catalogue file; the fourth execution-boundary component is gone.
- Tags and incidents are one ledger group, not a second store; the artefact registry is the `artefact` table; report, export, import and purge are commands, not record components.
- The guard has its seats on every crossing in one file; every other file states the rule instead of redrawing it.
- The transition table is drawn once, complete, and the human-surface strip lists touchpoints without redrawing transitions.
- The runtime key has one path (X5, X3, X11) and no direct edge from the host store into a sandbox.

## 5. Gaps the reviews could not close from the material

- The outbox intent's states (`pending`, `sending`, `reconciled`, `failed`, `superseded`), the ambiguous-send reconciliation and the same-key refusal of R-T-11 are Layer 3 matter and are not drawn at any layer that exists; the reliability property with the most crash cases is unreviewed until the outbox's Layer 3 section is written.
- `docs/design/TICKETS.md`, named by `milestones.md` as the build order inside a step, does not exist, so whether a fold is cheaper to build than two boxes cannot be judged against it.
- Appendix A maps rows to blocks, not to parts; a part-to-row column in each derivation table would let a reviewer test "no Milestone A part without a Milestone A row" mechanically.
