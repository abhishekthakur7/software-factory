# S0 Intake

Section 4 of the [Software Factory PRD](prd.md), one file per stage. The index holds the version, the reading rules, the rubric-line conventions, and the map from section numbers to files.

**Produces.** Fields on the `ticket` row: data class, trust-profile and approval-set hashes, service tier, ticket type, provisional tier, scrutiny requested, reviewer-set summary, and pilot-eligibility result. No agent-authored artefact file; the governed ticket source is registered. S0 is scripts except the scrutiny draft, which configuration can switch off.

| # | Requirement | Cites | Version | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-S0-1 | Mechanical gate, no LLM: the source ticket has non-empty acceptance criteria, a named owner, and a linked parent or Confluence page, read through `ticket-types.yaml`. Missing items and epics are rejected with the reason named. Before storage or inference, R-T-9 classifies and redacts the permitted source fields into `ticket_source`; "verbatim" means faithful after governed redaction, not an unfiltered Jira payload. S0 reads Jira through a least-privilege credential held by its trusted adapter, never mounted into an agent sandbox. | P1, P6, P7, FM-06, FM-16 | Initial | Script test per missing item, rejection reason, data class, and redaction fixture |
| R-S0-2 | Lookups, all from section 8 tables: service tier from `service-tiers.yaml`, failing with "service not tiered" when the service is absent (the factory never guesses a tier); ticket type from the Jira issue-type mapping; provisional tier from the tier matrix, service tier by ticket type. | D11, P1, P7, FM-05 | Initial | Script test over the full matrix and every unmapped input |
| R-S0-5 | Sensitive paths (authentication, authorisation, payments, secrets, schema migrations, infrastructure) force at least Heavy and add the configured path owner, distinct from the ticket engineer, to the planned reviewer set. Matching is provisional on ticket text and S1/S3 scope and authoritative on the S5 actual diff. Initial rejects such a ticket under R-S0-8; after graduation it may proceed only when the distinct owner approves at both S3 and S6. | P5, P10, FM-02, FM-14, FM-18 | Initial | Candidate/final detection, Initial exclusion, identity distinction, and Later-required routing tests |
| R-S0-6 | Scrutiny requested: when the draft is enabled, the agent drafts one paragraph from the ticket type and title, naming what the reviewer should look hardest at and what they may take on the evidence; when disabled (`scrutiny_draft` in `project.yaml`), the engineer writes it in the list view and the manifest's S0 agent, skill, and model entries are null. The action fails closed if any authority, expiry, source scope, or route is invalid; a human cannot override that or an Initial exclusion here. The acting role is recorded and a tier override carries its tag. | D11, D21, P1, P10, FM-09, FM-05, FM-24 | Initial | Eligibility, governance-validity, role, override, and non-override tests |
| R-S0-8 | Initial pilot eligibility is one T2 Java service and `small_feature`, with exactly one repository and target service. It excludes sensitive paths; authentication or permission behavior; payments or secrets; schema/data migration; infrastructure; regulated or disallowed data; new or changed public API, event, or serialized-data contracts; and any plan that depends on unavailable production facts. Detection at S0 rejects mechanically; discovery at S1 or S3, or proof at S5 that the required change needs an excluded surface, closes `pilot_excluded`. An accidental excluded path in the diff must be removed in S4 before S5 can pass. Widening any dimension is a recorded graduation decision under R-O-13, not a tier override. | D32, P1, P7, P10, FM-05, FM-14, FM-15 | Initial | Eligibility matrix, accidental-diff removal, and required-surface exclusion tests |

**Rubric, S0.** R-S0-1, R-S0-2, R-S0-5: deterministic, original. R-S0-6: grader, original.

**Human touchpoint.** One decision: eligible or not, with the type confirmed, an optional tier override, and the scrutiny paragraph. Target under two minutes for a Light ticket.
