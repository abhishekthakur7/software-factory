# S5 Cleanup pass

Section 4 of the [Software Factory PRD](prd.md), one file per stage. The index holds the version, the reading rules, the rubric-line conventions, and the map from section numbers to files.

Two tiers (D16). The blocking tier is deterministic and runs first. S5 never edits the ticket source or branch; repository-controlled recipes write only to R-I-14's disposable build layers, and factory scripts write governed evidence. The advisory tier is Later and never runs on a red build.

**Rubric, S5.** The blocking tier is the rubric: R-S5-1, R-S5-2, R-S3-12, R-S5-4, R-S5-5, R-S5-10, R-S5-12, R-S5-13, and the final-ownership/approval check in R-S6-6, deterministic throughout. Synthesis S5 and synthesis-factories finding 5 support the ordering; synthesis headline 7 supports contract-drift detection and its stated limits; synthesis headline 3 supports the size gate.

**Human touchpoint.** After the fix rounds of R-S4-9 are exhausted or do not apply, when the blocking tier fails or reports a blind spot: the engineer sends the ticket back, abandons, or records an authorised waiver under R-S5-13. Nothing is fixed by hand outside a stage run.
