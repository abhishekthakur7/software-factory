| R-S6-3 | On the first cycle no branch or diff is pushed before full review quorum; on a revision cycle the existing remote branch and PR remain unchanged until reapproval. **Rubric, S6.** R-I-12 on the packet, R-S6-1, R-S6-2, R-S6-6's race guard, and R-S6-7: deterministic. Synthesis S6 and synthesis-factories S6.

**Human touchpoint.** Each required reviewer approves or requests changes; an ordinary Initial ticket has one. This is the second structural gate. S6 reviews conformance to an approved design, not the design. Reviewers read the checks and the risk map first and open the diff at the three named places. The wait is measured on the `packet_approval` item (R-H-1).
