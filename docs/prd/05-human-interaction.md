# 5. Human interaction requirements

Part of the [Software Factory PRD](prd.md). The index holds the version, the reading rules, and the map from section numbers to files.

| # | Requirement | Cites | Version | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-H-1 | One queue holds every human item: questions, eligibility, plan and packet approval, failed or blind-spot checks, escalations, manual pauses, PR revisions and outcomes, rubric inspections; Later bloat signals and close surveys. It records queue latency and shows the minimum governed context. `pr_outcome` is non-blocking and excluded from attention/touchpoint measures. Queue latency is never labelled active attention. | D17, P1, FM-07, FM-09 | Initial | Script test per kind and metric inclusion; seeded list view |
| R-H-3 | The factory never interrupts. The digest uses a transactional outbox on the configured cadence, groups open items by ticket and age, and sends nothing for an empty queue. Under R-T-9 it contains only ticket identifier, tier, item kind, age, and local command/link; no ticket text, code, question options, artefact content, or secret. It is the only Slack write. | D17, P1, P7, FM-07, FM-09, FM-24 | Initial | Governance, outbox, empty-queue, and duplicate-post tests |
| R-H-4 | The local list view takes all human actions: eligibility including data class; question/default and flag override; criteria-plan and packet approval/redirect with semantic verdicts and one immutable record per required slot; policy-permitted blind-spot waiver; base refresh or factory-manifest migration; pause/resume/stop; escalation; send-back; rubric inspection; manual PR revision/outcome; production exposure and coverage; incident/control event and attribution/disposition; packet-defect or policy-exception tag and defect-resolution evidence; abandon and purge. It records canonical actor and RACI role. Acting resumes only when quorum and other guards permit; inspection never silently advances. It opens governed artefacts and never exposes a credential. | D17, D15, C5, P1, P10, FM-07, FM-09, FM-21, FM-25 | Initial | Script test per action, approval slot, incident/control action, resolution, and role |
| R-H-8 | Every packet and every question is self-contained: a reader with no access to any transcript can decide from the item alone. Failure is a factory defect: for a plan/review decision the human records a `packet_defect` tag with FM-10 on the exact `approval_record`, and for a question on its exact version, on approve/answer as well as on request changes. A later corrected packet or question appends explicit resolution evidence without erasing the defect. Escalation items are held to the same rule through the `failure_history` schema. | P9, FM-10 | Initial | Engineer's reading of each item (temporary); exact-binding and resolution tests; grader in the observer pass when it runs (R-O-10) |
| R-H-10 | One-way sync of human-facing artefacts, brief, plan, decisions, packet, to the Confluence user space (D22). | D22, P9, FM-10 | Later | Script test |
| R-H-11 | On first draft-PR creation, Initial queues one non-blocking `pr_outcome` item. A human may record a requested revision with review ground and target stage; this writes `revision_after_approval`, invalidates final approval, and returns the local branch through normal S3/S4/S5/S6 while the remote branch remains unchanged until an approved `pr_update`. The final action records observed `merged` or `abandoned`, final PR head and target-base SHAs, final PR-body hash from a governed snapshot or immutable locator, merge SHA when merged, required-check disposition (`green`, `waived` with reason, `red`, or `unknown`), actor role and observation time. External truth is never refused or rewritten: if head, base, checks or body differs from the latest approved subject, the action still records the outcome, sets `approval_disposition = mismatched`, and appends both a required FM-25 `control_defect` tag and an approval-binding control-defect event with policy-derived severity; a disposition row starts `open`. A post-hoc exception cannot make it approved. A match records `matched`; unavailable evidence records `unknown`. Independently, abandonment appends production coverage `not_deployed`; merge appends `unknown` until deployment/exposure start and source are recorded, after which a human may attest `none_observed` through a stated time or append a production-incident event with its required tag. Event roots are never superseded; coverage and disposition versions supersede only within their own series. The runner performs no Initial polling; narrowly scoped outbox reconciliation reads are allowed. S7 later replaces, not duplicates, remote observation. | C5, D20, D25, P7, P8, P10, FM-17, FM-18, FM-25 | Initial | Revision/create-update, body-binding, actual-outcome, mismatched-control/tag, exposure/coverage, incident-event, state, and no-poll tests |
| R-H-12 | Every plan and packet `approval_record`, including redirect/reject decisions, requires the acting human's coarse active-attention bucket from 2.2, including `unknown`; non-approval queue actions may record one optionally. Reports keep it separate from queue latency and decision outcome. The factory collects no keystrokes, focus events, editor telemetry, or inferred individual-attention score. | P1, P11, FM-09 | Initial | Multi-reviewer view and forbidden-telemetry manifest/code tests |
| R-H-13 | Every stage and S4 attempt has visible start/end boundaries. Status shows stage, attempt, elapsed wall clock, budget remaining, current registered outputs, and whether a pause is pending. A human may request pause at the next boundary, stop immediately, inspect completed output, or send back from a pause; no routine approval is added. `advance` checks the durable pause flag before starting each boundary. | C5, P1, P10, P11, FM-09, FM-21 | Initial | Boundary race, durable pause, resume, stop, and send-back tests |
